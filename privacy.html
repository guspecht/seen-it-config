Seen It privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Seen It is a browser extension made by Gustavo Specht (ABN 11 827 021 139), seenit@guspecht.com.au.

What the extension stores

Seen It saves the titles you mark (title name, status, the streaming site's id for the title, and dates), the dates from any viewing history you import, and your settings. This data is stored only in your browser using the browser's extension storage. It is not sent to us. The only exception is the show names used for new season alerts, described below.

Viewing history import

When you choose to import your Netflix viewing history, the extension reads the titles shown on your Netflix viewing activity page, or a CSV file you select, and saves them in your browser as described above. Nothing is uploaded.

New season alerts (Seen It Pro)

If you use new season alerts, the extension sends the names of the shows you follow to TVmaze (tvmaze.com) once a day to look up episode dates. TVmaze receives those show names and your IP address, as with any website request, under its own privacy policy. No other information is sent, and you can turn alerts off for any show in the extension's settings.

Health checks (optional)

If you turn on "Send anonymous health checks", the extension tells us whether each part of a streaming site still works with Seen It, so we can fix problems quickly when a site changes its layout. Each report contains only the extension version, the layout configuration version, your browser language, the part of the site checked (for example "posters"), whether it worked, and which layout version matched. It never includes titles, viewing history, ids or anything that identifies you. Reports are stored in a Google Sheet that only we can access. You can turn this off at any time in Settings, Advanced.

The extension also downloads a small layout configuration file from us every few hours. That download contains no information about you.

Year in Review images

Year in Review images are created inside your browser. They are only shared if you download or copy them yourself.

Payments

If you buy Seen It Pro, payment is handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe. They receive your email address and payment details under their own privacy policies. We receive your email address and payment status so we can unlock Pro features. We never see your card details.

Seen It Pro waitlist

If you choose to join the Seen It Pro waitlist, you fill in a form hosted by Tally (tally.so), outside the extension. It asks for your email address and, optionally, which streaming services you use. We use them only to tell you when Seen It Pro opens and about any early-bird offer. We never sell or share them; Tally stores the sign-ups for us under its own privacy policy. You can ask to be removed at any time by emailing seenit@guspecht.com.au.

What we don't do

We don't collect browsing history, sell data, show ads, or use analytics. The extension itself never asks for your email address: the only places it is collected are the optional waitlist form and, if you buy Pro, the payment checkout. Waitlist email addresses are never sold or shared.

Deleting your data

Use "Delete all marks and settings" in the extension's options, or uninstall the extension. To be removed from the Pro waitlist, or to delete your payment account, email seenit@guspecht.com.au.
